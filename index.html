About Me

Screenwriter, 3D Artist, Musician, Dancer

Art is storytelling. I am an artist who shows the world visions that cannot be accessed through mundane means; albeit through music, digital art and film, writing, and movement. I resurrect what’s buried beneath the subconscious into life, uncovering stories that invoke your memory. We all lived them. Some simply choose to forget; some even refuse to talk about. My approach to art is not so much about brushes and strokes of randomness, but daggers of intention.
