  1. THE QUIET ARCHIVE Written by AMAROK Draft 3 · 2026

    01

    The Quiet Archive

    • Feature
    • Psychological thriller
    • 104 pages

    A night archivist discovers the city's erased records are still being written, and that tomorrow's entry has her name on it.

    In development Read script →

    Mara Voss has catalogued the city's records alone, at night, for eleven years. When a ledger appears with tomorrow's date and her name in fresh ink, she follows the entries backwards through decades of erased lives, and finds that every person who was written into the archive's last page disappeared the following day.

    FADE IN:

    INT. CITY HALL ARCHIVE - BASEMENT - NIGHT

    Endless shelves. A single green lamp. Dust hangs in the light like it has nowhere else to be.

    MARA VOSS (30s), cardigan over a security badge, wheels a cart of ledgers between the stacks. She stops. A drawer is open that wasn't a minute ago.

    She slides it closed. Somewhere deeper in the dark, another drawer slides open.

    MARA

    (quietly)

    Hello?

    Nothing. She pulls a ledger from the open drawer. The ink on the last page is still wet.

    INSERT - THE LEDGER

    Tomorrow's date. And beneath it, in careful handwriting: MARA VOSS.

  2. SALT & STATIC Written by AMAROK Final draft · 2025

    02

    Salt & Static

    • Short
    • Drama
    • 14 pages

    Two estranged brothers rebuild their late father's pirate radio station, broadcasting the one song he never let them hear.

    Completed Read script →

    Eight years after walking out, Noah comes home for his father's funeral and finds his younger brother Eli restoring the pirate radio station their father ran from a cliffside shack. To finish it, they have to play the tapes he left behind, including the one song he swore they would never hear.

    EXT. CLIFFSIDE SHACK - DAWN

    A rusted antenna leans into the wind above a shack held together by tarp and stubbornness.

    INT. SHACK - CONTINUOUS

    Dust sheets over everything. ELI (20s) pulls one off: a homemade radio desk, every dial taped and labelled in a father's handwriting.

    NOAH (30s) lingers in the doorway. He hasn't crossed a threshold in this house in eight years.

    NOAH

    He kept all of it.

    ELI

    He kept everything. Except us.

    Eli flips a switch. The desk hums. A small red light blinks: ON AIR.

  3. GHOST LIGHT Written by AMAROK Draft 2 · 2026

    03

    Ghost Light

    • Pilot
    • Mystery / horror
    • 58 pages

    When a shuttered theatre reopens, its stage manager realises the lamp left burning each night was never meant to keep the ghosts away.

    In development Read script →

    The Meridian Theatre has been dark for thirty years. Stage manager June Okafor is hired to bring it back to life, and the first rule she's given is the oldest one in theatre: never let the ghost light go out. Over one season, she learns who the light was really keeping in.

    INT. THE MERIDIAN THEATRE - STAGE - NIGHT

    Rows of empty velvet seats. On the bare stage stands a single caged bulb on a pole: the ghost light.

    JUNE OKAFOR (40s), stage manager, keys on a lanyard, checks the bulb. Warm. Steady.

    She turns to leave. Behind her, the bulb DIMS.

    JUNE

    (without turning)

    Not tonight.

    The light steadies.

    From the back of the house: slow applause. One pair of hands.

    CUT TO BLACK.

  4. WHAT THE RIVER KEPT Written by AMAROK Draft 1 · 2026

    04

    What the River Kept

    • Feature
    • Coming of age
    • 96 pages

    The summer the river dries up, a boy finds the town's buried history on its bed, and a reason everyone wanted it drowned.

    Writing Read script →

    In the driest summer on record, the river through Miller's Bend disappears, and twelve-year-old Tommy starts finding things on its bed that the town spent fifty years pretending never happened. With his best friend Callie, he digs up the truth the adults drowned, one object at a time.

    EXT. MILLER'S BEND - RIVERBED - DAY

    Cracked mud stretches where the river used to be. Fish bones. A bicycle wheel. A church bell, half buried, green with age.

    TOMMY (12) crouches and digs at something with a stick. A wooden box. Lead-sealed.

    CALLIE (12) skids down the bank behind him.

    CALLIE

    My dad says don't touch anything down here.

    TOMMY

    Why?

    CALLIE

    He didn't say. He just said it like he meant it.

  5. ROOM 9 Written by AMAROK Final draft · 2024

    05

    Room 9

    • Short
    • Surreal drama
    • 5 pages

    A hotel housekeeper who hasn't spoken in forty years slips a note under the one door no guest may open, and gets an answer in her missing sister's handwriting.

    Completed Read script →

    Ada has cleaned the third floor of the Grand Alder Hotel for decades without saying a word. When she slips a note under the door of Room 9, the room no guest may book, the replies come back in the handwriting of June, the sister who vanished from this hotel in 1984, writing as if it's still the night before she disappeared. Ada has one chance to find her voice and ask June to stay.

    INT. GRAND ALDER HOTEL - THIRD FLOOR CORRIDOR - NIGHT

    A housekeeping cart rolls over patterned carpet. ADA (50s) pushes it without a sound.

    She passes Rooms 7 and 8. Room 9 has no number on the door. Just the faint shadow where one used to be.

    Ada stops. Takes a folded note from her apron. Slides it under the door.

    She waits.

    The note slides back out. Unfolded. Something new is written beneath hers.
